# Account FAQ

## Resetting your password

Click "Forgot password" on the login page. A reset link is emailed to you and
expires after 1 hour. If you don't receive it, check spam, then contact
support@example.com.

## Changing your email address

Go to Settings > Account > Email. You must confirm the change from both the
old and new email addresses within 24 hours.

## Two-factor authentication

We support authenticator apps (TOTP) and SMS. Enable 2FA under
Settings > Security. Backup codes are shown once when you enable 2FA —
store them somewhere safe.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is permanent. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
Your data is removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.
