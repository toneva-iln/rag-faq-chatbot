# Billing FAQ

## Understanding your bill

Your monthly bill includes your subscription fee plus any usage-based charges.
Bills are issued on the 1st of each month and are due within 15 days.

## Payment methods

We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), ACH bank transfer, and PayPal.
To update your payment method, go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods.

## Refunds

Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for annual plans.
Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel anytime and keep access
until the end of the billing period. To request a refund, email
billing@example.com with your account email and invoice number.

## Late payments

If payment fails, we retry 3 times over 7 days. After that, your account is
downgraded to the free tier. No data is deleted for 90 days.
